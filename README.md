# 🏥 Healthcare Data Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes healthcare data to identify key patterns and
business insights related to patient demographics, medical conditions,
admission types, healthcare billing, and insurance providers.

An interactive dashboard was developed using Microsoft Excel with
PivotTables, PivotCharts, and data visualization techniques.

The objective is to transform healthcare data into meaningful insights
that can support healthcare management, resource planning, and
data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering the following key questions:

- Which medical conditions have the highest number of cases?
- How do medical conditions vary across age groups?
- How do healthcare conditions differ by gender?
- Which admission type has the highest number of cases?
- Which medical conditions have the highest Emergency admissions?
- Which medical conditions generate the highest billing?
- Which insurance provider contributes the highest billing?
- Where are the major healthcare demand and billing concentrations?

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Demographic Analysis

The dashboard analyzes medical conditions across:

- Gender
- Age Category
- Medical Condition
- Blood Type

The analysis helps identify differences in healthcare conditions
across demographic groups.

### 2. Admission Analysis

Admission patterns are analyzed across:

- Emergency
- Urgent
- Elective

Medical conditions are compared to identify areas with higher
demand for immediate medical attention.

### 3. Billing Analysis

Healthcare billing is analyzed by:

- Admission Type
- Medical Condition
- Insurance Provider

This helps identify the conditions and admission categories with
higher reported billing.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🚨 Admission Insights

- **Emergency admissions account for 35.87% of all cases**, making
  Emergency the largest admission category.

- **Urgent admissions account for 33.36% of cases.**

- **Emergency and Urgent admissions together represent 69.23% of
  all cases**, highlighting significant demand for timely medical care.

- **Hypertension records the highest number of Emergency admissions
  with 993 cases.**

- **Asthma records the highest Urgent admissions with 820 cases.**

- **Obesity has the highest Elective admissions with 925 cases.**

### 💰 Billing Insights

- **Total reported billing is 233.32M.**

- **Emergency admissions generate the highest billing at 86.91M,
  followed by Urgent admissions at 75.67M.**

- **Cancer has the highest total reported billing among the medical
  conditions at 65.33M.**

- **Medicare has the highest billing among insurance providers at
  55.56M.**

### 👥 Demographic Insights

- **Female patients account for 5,075 records (50.75%), while male
  patients account for 4,925 records (49.25%).**

- **Senior Citizens represent the largest age category with 3,661
  records (36.61%).**

- **Hypertension has the highest number of reported cases among the
  six medical conditions, with 2,155 cases.**

- Medical-condition patterns vary across age groups and genders,
  highlighting the importance of demographic analysis in healthcare
  planning.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following areas could be considered for
healthcare planning:

1. Strengthen Emergency and Urgent care capacity because these
   admission categories account for a large proportion of cases.

2. Focus preventive healthcare and early-detection initiatives on
   conditions associated with higher Emergency admissions.

3. Monitor high-billing conditions such as Cancer to support
   financial and resource planning.

4. Pay particular attention to the Senior Citizen population because
   it represents the largest age category in the dataset.

5. Use demographic and admission patterns to support targeted
   healthcare resource allocation.
